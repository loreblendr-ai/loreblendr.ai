# App Installation

LoreBlendr.AI is available on the Apple App Store for iPhone, iPad, and Mac with Apple silicon (M-series chips).

[**Install from the App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/loreblendr-ai/id6747638829)

## iPhone and iPad

1.  Open the **App Store** on your device.
2.  Search for "LoreBlendr.AI".
3.  Tap the **Get** button or the cloud icon to install the app.

## Mac (Apple Silicon M1, M2, M3, etc.)

Macs with Apple M-series chips can run the iPad version of LoreBlendr.AI.

1.  Open the **App Store** on your Mac.
2.  Search for "LoreBlendr.AI".
3.  Above the search results, click on the **"iPhone & iPad Apps"** tab.
4.  Find LoreBlendr.AI in the list and click the **Get** button or the cloud icon to install.

The app will be available in your Applications folder and will run in iPad compatibility mode.
