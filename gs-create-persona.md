# Create and Manage Personas

Personas define the "voice" you use when chatting with characters. You can create personas for different scenarios.

## Selecting a Persona

On the **Manage Personas** screen, you can choose which persona to use for new chats. The selected persona is highlighted in blue. You can select one of your custom personas or even use an imported character as your persona.

![Viewing and selecting a persona](assets/loreblendr/screenshots/persona-view.png)

## Creating a New Persona

1.  Navigate to **Manage Personas**.
2.  Tap the **+** button to add a new persona.
3.  Fill in the details for your persona and save.

Tip: Use any AI chat to create a persona and image. You can even use a character designed to create personas.

![Editing a persona](assets/loreblendr/screenshots/edit-persona.png)


## Editing or Deleting a Persona

To edit or delete a persona you've created, **long-press** on it in the list to open the context menu.

![Long Pressing a persona](assets/loreblendr/screenshots/long-press-persona.jpg)
