# Create and Manage Personas

Personas define the "voice" you use when chatting with characters. You can create multiple personas for different scenarios.

## Creating a New Persona

1.  Navigate to the **Settings** tab and tap **Manage Personas**.
2.  Tap the **+** button to add a new persona.
3.  Fill in the details for your persona and save.

![Editing a persona](assets/loreblendr/screenshots/edit-persona.png)

## Selecting a Persona

On the **Manage Personas** screen, you can choose which persona to use for your chats. The selected persona is highlighted in blue. You can select one of your custom personas or even use an imported character as your persona.

![Viewing and selecting a persona](assets/loreblendr/screenshots/persona-view.png)

## Editing or Deleting a Persona

To edit or delete a persona you've created, **long-press** on it in the list to open the context menu.
